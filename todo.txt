# CAR RENTAL MANAGEMENT ERP
## Complete Car Rental, Self-Drive, Chauffeur-Driven & Fleet Management Software
### Laravel + PHP + MySQL | Production-Ready Enterprise Application

---

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior Laravel software architect, car rental business consultant, fleet management specialist, vehicle operations expert, financial systems developer, database architect, and enterprise UI/UX designer.

Your task is to design and develop a complete, production-ready Car Rental Management ERP using Laravel and MySQL.

The application must manage the complete lifecycle of a car rental business, from vehicle registration and customer booking to vehicle allocation, rental agreement, vehicle handover, trip management, return inspection, billing, payment collection, maintenance, and fleet profitability.

This must be a complete operational ERP, not simply a car listing website or online booking application.

The system should support:

1. Self-drive car rental.
2. Chauffeur-driven car rental.
3. Hourly car rental.
4. Daily car rental.
5. Weekly car rental.
6. Monthly car rental.
7. Long-term vehicle leasing.
8. Airport pickup and drop.
9. Railway station pickup and drop.
10. Outstation rentals.
11. Local city rentals.
12. Corporate car rentals.
13. Wedding and event rentals.
14. Tourist vehicle rentals.
15. Subscription-based rentals.
16. Commercial fleet rentals.

Primary market: India.

Design the application for independent rental businesses, multi-branch rental agencies, travel companies, fleet operators, airport rental companies, and corporate vehicle rental providers.

# 39. IMPORTANT BUSINESS LOGIC AND VALIDATIONS

These rules are mandatory.

## 39.1 Vehicle Availability

- One vehicle cannot have overlapping confirmed bookings.
- Active rentals must block availability.
- Maintenance must block availability.
- Return inspection must be completed before a vehicle becomes available.
- Vehicle documents must satisfy configured requirements.
- Vehicle location must be considered when allocating bookings.
- All availability checks must be transaction-safe.

## 39.2 Booking Integrity

- Every booking must have a customer.
- Every confirmed booking must have a valid price snapshot.
- Every booking must have pickup and return details.
- Vehicle allocation must be validated.
- Driver assignment must be validated.
- Booking cancellation must preserve history.
- Extensions must check future reservations.
- Discounts above the authorized limit require approval.

## 39.3 Rental Integrity

- A rental cannot start without required handover completion.
- A rental cannot be completed without a return record.
- Odometer readings must be recorded.
- Fuel levels must be recorded.
- Damage must be documented.
- Extra charges must have supporting evidence.
- Security deposit refunds must be reconciled.
- Vehicle status must be updated after settlement.

## 39.4 Financial Integrity

- Security deposits are liabilities, not income.
- Every payment must be traceable.
- Every refund must reference an original transaction.
- Invoice totals must be calculated from line items.
- Tax must follow configured rules.
- Posted accounting entries must balance.
- Financial records must not be silently deleted.

## 39.5 Transaction Safety

Use database transactions and locking for:

- Booking confirmation.
- Vehicle allocation.
- Driver assignment.
- Rental start.
- Rental return.
- Extension approval.
- Deposit settlement.
- Invoice generation.
- Payment allocation.
- Refunds.

Use idempotency protection to prevent duplicate bookings and payments.
